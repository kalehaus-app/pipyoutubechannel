# Character Bible

The channel's long-term goal is that children care about **Pip and Teddy**,
not merely the activity in the title. Consistency across episodes is what
builds that attachment, so treat this file as binding.

## Pip

The main character.

- Toddler boy, curly brown hair
- Huge sparkly expressive eyes, rosy cheeks
- Sunny-yellow romper with **Pip** in red bubble letters on the chest
- Soul ID `44f638c0-b402-424a-a6ba-962eae4ca86d`

Personality: extremely curious, sweet, helpful, playful, slightly
mischievous, easily excited, very expressive. Sometimes nervous before trying
something new — ultimately brave. Loves animals, vehicles, water, food and new
experiences. Deeply attached to Teddy. Trusts and loves Mommy.

**Pip should not perform everything perfectly.** His mistakes and
overreactions are the charm: too much sunscreen, shoes on the wrong feet,
a silly face at new food, getting sprayed by water, making a bigger mess while
helping, a float that spins backward.

### Recurring signature behaviours

Reuse these deliberately — they are how a toddler recognises Pip:

- Eyes widen dramatically when he gets an idea
- Squeezes Teddy when nervous
- Little two-foot celebration bounce
- Holds Teddy overhead when victorious
- Worried "Oh, Teddy!" reaction when Teddy gets into trouble

## Teddy

Pip's best friend and comfort object.

- Small plush bear, red-and-blue body, soft stuffed-toy proportions
- **Not a Soul.** Describe him directly in every prompt.
- Normally does not speak

Teddy works as comfort object, emotional companion, comedy device, and
occasional story catalyst: Teddy gets splashed, a goat grabs Teddy, Teddy is
buried under laundry, Teddy goes down the slide first, Teddy gets his own seat
belt, Teddy floats away, Teddy is covered in flour.

Use Teddy often. Pip and Teddy should feel increasingly inseparable.

## Mommy

- Grown adult woman, early 30s, tall
- Brown shoulder-length hair, cozy sweater / warm casual clothing
- Clearly a **full adult**
- Soul ID `6c889761-3c6d-45ee-8425-e6cb87dedeff`

Mommy is Pip's secure home base. She guides, reassures, laughs with him,
accompanies him, opens and closes adventures, and helps with daily habits.

**In long-form, Pip should often act for himself rather than having Mommy
solve every problem.** Attachment comes from watching Pip try.

## Casting rules — hard

Unless an episode explicitly requires another approved character:

- Exactly **one adult**, exactly **one toddler**; Teddy may also appear
- No duplicate Pip, no duplicate Mommy
- No random children, no background bystanders unless truly necessary
- Mommy must be proportionally adult-sized; Pip must stay toddler-sized

Mommy drifting toward child proportions is the most common generation
failure. Every prompt containing both characters must say so explicitly —
"his clearly adult Mommy… much taller and older than Pip" — and should anchor
the Soul on Mommy rather than Pip.

## Text rule

The only text anywhere on screen is **Pip** on the romper. No generated signs,
labels, captions, logos or stray lettering. State this in every prompt; image
models invent text unprompted.

## Visual language

Warm, cozy, wholesome, colorful. Soft high-end 3D animated-family-film look,
expressive faces, rounded child-friendly forms, golden natural light where it
fits, clean compositions, toddler-safe environments.

Outdoor adventures: saturated but pleasant colors, blue skies, big readable
objects, strong depth, obvious foreground/midground/background action.

**One clear visual idea per shot.**

## Comedy rule

Physical comedy is what makes this content perform.

- Every short scene: ideally one memorable beat
- Every major long-form sequence: roughly 2–3 "money beats"

Surprise splash, Teddy falling somewhere harmless, Pip overdoing something, an
object rolling away, Pip spinning backward, food landing somewhere silly, a
bubble explosion, a pile collapsing, Pip ducking one thing and getting hit by
another, Mommy reacting before Pip realises what is coming.

Comedy stays harmless, wholesome, and visually obvious to a toddler.
